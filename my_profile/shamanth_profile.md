### 🌍 Economic Forecasting & PPP Analysis
**Business Forecasting Project · Python · statsmodels**
[GitHub](https://github.com/shamanth-g-bharadwaj/Economic-forecasting-ppp-analysis)

> **Elevator Pitch:** A rigorous time series analysis project testing whether Purchasing Power Parity holds between Switzerland and Singapore, using 26 years of monthly macroeconomic data. After stationarity testing, OLS-based PPP testing, and Box-Jenkins ARIMA model selection, I found that neither absolute nor relative PPP holds in this pair — and that **ARIMA(0,1,1)** best describes the real exchange rate path, with a stable but widening forecast horizon.

**What makes it stand out:** The project is methodologically disciplined — every modelling decision is grounded in statistical testing rather than assumption. ADF testing, Ljung-Box residual diagnostics, and AIC/BIC comparison were used systematically to justify model selection. The finding that PPP does not hold is itself analytically interesting, and the project demonstrates comfort with economic theory, time series mechanics, and the limits of models.

**Tech:** Python · pandas · statsmodels · ARIMA · ADF Testing · OLS · matplotlib · Jupyter Notebook
